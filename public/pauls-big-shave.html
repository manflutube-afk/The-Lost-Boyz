Paul’s Big Shave

Saturday 19 September 2026, 7pm

The Ship Inn, Lerryn

Free entry · everyone welcome

A year ago Paul stopped shaving. On Saturday the beard comes off, in the bar, in front of everyone — and every penny raised goes to Brake, the road safety charity.

We are playing through the evening while it happens. Two of us, the full set, and a decent racket in a good pub. Come along, have a pint, have a listen, and put something in the pot if you can.

Brake matters to the pair of us more than most causes do. They are the people who support families after a road crash, and they were there for ours. That is why we are playing this one for nothing.

Coming along?

Let the lads know on Facebook so we have some idea of numbers, and if you cannot make the night itself you can still back Paul from wherever you are.
