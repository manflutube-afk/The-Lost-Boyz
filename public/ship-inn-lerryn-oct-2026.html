The Ship Inn, Lerryn

Saturday 17 October 2026, 8pm

The Ship Inn, Fore Street, Lerryn, near Lostwithiel, Cornwall PL22 0PT

Everyone welcome

We are back at The Ship Inn for the night. Two of us, the full set, and a proper Saturday in a proper pub down by the water.

Classic rock, played loud enough to enjoy and not so loud you cannot get a word in at the bar. Come along, bring whoever you like, have a pint and a listen. If you have not seen us before, this is a good one to start with.

The best thing you can do for a band like us is simply turn up — and tell the landlord you came for the music.
