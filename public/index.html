Our Story

Darren Endean

Acoustic & electric guitars · Vocals

Writes the songs, plays the strings — lead, rhythm and bass — and draws the artwork. If it has a fretboard on it, that is Darren.

Andrew Boraston

Percussion & drums · Vocals

The engine room, and the other half of the harmonies. Co-produced the single.

Two next-door neighbours and lifelong friends, both of whom lost their sons. The band exists because of them.
